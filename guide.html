02光敏展示：一片雲飛過校園

麵包板上排 13 顆光敏電阻，各代表一棟大樓。拿一張 A4 紙慢慢從左掃到右，就像一片雲飄過學校上空：

  • 被遮住的大樓上方出現雲影，屋頂太陽能發電下降
  • 變暗的大樓自動補光：「開關」模式低於門檻就全亮、亮起金框；「調光補償」模式越暗開得越亮（顯示 ◐ 調光 xx%）
  • 左側「全日負載曲線」「各棟用電排行」和下方「告警與事件」跟著即時變化

需要的東西

  • Arduino Mega 2560（已燒入網頁控制台程式 mega_io.ino）＋ USB 線
  • 大麵包板、13 顆光敏電阻、13 顆 10kΩ 電阻、杜邦線
  • 筆電（Chrome 或 Edge，要能上網）、一張 A4 紙

接線（每一顆都一樣）

5V ── 光敏電阻 ──┬── A0（A1、A2 … A12）
                  └── 10kΩ ── GND

光敏電阻在麵包板上由左到右依序接 A0 到 A12，順序和戰情室地圖由左到右的大樓相同：

腳位A0A1A2A3A4A5A6
大樓工程一館工程三館資訊館雲青館智管三館智管一館教學大樓
腳位A7A8A9A10A11A12
大樓圖資館創媒二館研究大樓創媒一館行政中心體育館
用三腳的「光敏模組」也可以：VCC 接 5V、GND 接 GND、AO 接 A0～A12，控制台的「遮住時」改選「數值變大」。

操作步驟

  1. 打開網頁控制台（已預設好展示模式）：
    webhandyman996.github.io/mega-web-io/?mode=multi&room=show
  2. 按「連接 Mega 2560」，選 Mega 的 COM 埠。
  3. 紙先拿開，在現場燈光下按「校正亮度」。換場地、窗簾開關後要重按一次。
  4. 「照明」建議選「調光補償」：紙掃過時各棟照明用電平滑升降，比「開關」更好看。
  5. 按「開始上傳」，下方 13 格開始跳數字。
  6. 另開一個視窗，打開戰情室 ?room=show，投影到大螢幕。
  7. 拿 A4 紙由 A0 慢慢掃到 A12，約 5～10 秒走完。
沒帶板子時，控制台按「沒有板子，先試看看」，會自動模擬一片雲每 16 秒飄過一次。畫面上會標「模擬」。

可以這樣講

「這 13 顆光敏電阻就是 13 棟大樓屋頂的日照感測器。我現在拿紙當作一片雲——雲飄到哪裡，那棟大樓的太陽能就掉下來，教室變暗就自動開燈，整個校園的用電馬上反映在戰情室上。感測、判斷、控制，就是物聯網節能的三層架構。」
要誠實講清楚：光敏電阻讀到的是相對亮度（0～1023），不是正式的照度單位；開燈增加多少電、太陽能少多少，是依那棟大樓的模型推估，畫面上有標示。

02Light-sensor demo: a cloud passing over campus

Thirteen light-dependent resistors (LDRs) sit on a breadboard, one per campus building. Slowly sweep a sheet of A4 paper from left to right, like a cloud drifting over the campus:

  • A cloud shadow appears over each covered building and its rooftop solar output drops
  • Darkened buildings turn their lights on automatically: in "Switch" mode they go fully on below the threshold (gold outline); in "Dimming" mode the darker it gets, the brighter the lights (shown as ◐ dim xx%)
  • The daily load curve, the building ranking and the alerts panel all react in real time

What you need

  • Arduino Mega 2560 (with the web console firmware mega_io.ino) + USB cable
  • Large breadboard, 13 LDRs, 13 × 10 kΩ resistors, jumper wires
  • Laptop (Chrome or Edge, online) and a sheet of A4 paper

Wiring (the same for every sensor)

5V ── LDR ──┬── A0 (A1, A2 … A12)
             └── 10kΩ ── GND

Wire the LDRs left to right to A0 through A12, in the same order as the buildings on the map from left to right:

PinA0A1A2A3A4A5A6
BuildingEng 1Eng 3ITYunqingSLM 3SLM 1Teaching
PinA7A8A9A10A11A12
BuildingLibraryMedia 2ResearchMedia 1AdminGym
A 3-pin light-sensor module also works: VCC to 5V, GND to GND, AO to A0–A12, and set "When covered" to "Value rises" in the console.

Steps

  1. Open the web console (already set to demo mode):
    webhandyman996.github.io/mega-web-io/?mode=multi&room=show&lang=en
  2. Press "Connect Mega 2560" and pick the Mega's COM port.
  3. With the paper away, press "Calibrate" under the room lighting. Press it again after changing rooms or opening the curtains.
  4. For "Lighting", "Dimming" looks best: each building's lighting load rises and falls smoothly as the paper passes.
  5. Press "Start upload"; the 13 boxes below start updating.
  6. In another window, open the Energy Center at ?room=show&lang=en and project it on the big screen.
  7. Sweep the paper slowly from A0 to A12, taking about 5–10 seconds.
No board? Press "No board? Try the demo" in the console: a simulated cloud passes every 16 seconds, marked as a demo on screen.

What to say

"These 13 light sensors are the sunlight sensors on the roofs of 13 campus buildings. This sheet of paper is a cloud. Wherever it goes, that building's solar power drops, the classrooms get darker and the lights switch on, and the whole campus's electricity use shows up on the Energy Center right away. Sensing, deciding, acting: that is the three-layer architecture of IoT energy saving."
Be honest about the limits: the LDR reads relative brightness (0–1023), not a calibrated lux value. How much extra power the lights use and how much solar is lost are estimated from each building's model, and the screen labels them as such.
